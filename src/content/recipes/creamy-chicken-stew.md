steps:
  - "Add olive oil to the pan, cook chicken breasts over medium heat, season with salt and pepper"
  - "Chop celery, carrots and potatoes into bite sized pieces as desired and dice onion"
  - "Melt butter in pot over medium heat"
  - "Add celery, carrots, and onion and cook until onion is translucent"
  - "Mince garlic into pot and cook until fragrant"
  - "Deglaze the pot with white wine, let simmer"
  - "Add chicken broth and potatoes to pot and bring to a boil"
  - "Stir in Italian seasoning, paprika, thyme, rosemary, salt and pepper"
  - "Reduce heat and simmer until potatoes are fork tender, 15-20 minutes"
  - "Stir in 1 cup of heavy whipping cream, mix other cup with corn starch and remove lumps before adding to pot"
  - "Cut chicken into bite sized pieces and add to pot"
  - "Bring stew to boil for 5 minutes, stirring often until thick"
