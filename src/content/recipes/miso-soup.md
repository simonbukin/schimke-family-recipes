Ingredients:

- Vegetable broth, 4 cups
- Water, 3 cups
- Miso paste, 3 tbsp
- Firm tofu, 1 lb

Cookware:

- Pot, 1
- Sauce pan, 1
- Wooden spoon, 1

Steps:

- Drain and chop tofu into cubes
- Add vegetable broth to a pot and bring to boil
- Add miso paste
- Add tofu and boil until tofu is desired texture
